# Community conduct

Be precise, patient, and respectful. Discuss code and evidence rather than a
person's background or intent. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material and may restrict participation
when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository owner. Reports will be reviewed without retaliation and shared only
with people needed to resolve them.
